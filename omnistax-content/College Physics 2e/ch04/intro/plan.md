## What the page is

The chapter's own introduction, kept as a page of its own and listed before
4.1, as rule 21 asks. It is the book's five paragraphs on what dynamics is,
on Newton and Galileo, and on where classical mechanics stops holding, with
the boxed note Making Connections: Past and Present Philosophy kept as the
book prints it. The book sets no learning objectives, no summary and no
exercises here, so the page carries none of them, and it has no lead, since
nothing is written in the book's place. The one term the paragraphs mark,
dynamics, is already a glossary row of 4.1, so rule 21 leaves it there and
the page adds nothing to the glossary.
