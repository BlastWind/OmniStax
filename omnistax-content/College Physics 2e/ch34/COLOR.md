Prepared 2026-09-28 in the chapter's prep pass, beside `config.md`; brought
under root `RULES.md` item 7 on 2026-10-04, when only the introduction was
built. Colour reaches these pages in item 7's four ways, fact, convention,
referent and category, and where two apply the earlier wins. This file records
what is particular to the chapter; it invents no hue.

These stay in ink: $G$ and $H_0$, constants; a count of galaxies, a ratio to the
critical density, the fraction of dark matter.

A word or phrase wears a category when it names a particular one the reader can
point at: the 50 Mly beyond which the expansion is uniform, the 2.7 K of the
background radiation, the $T_\text{c}$ of 35 K announced in 1986, the eight
solar masses above which a neutron star must collapse. The kind in general
("critical temperature", "the critical density" as a definition), its unit and
its definition stay ink.

## Facts and conventions

The element palette carries every atom of 34.25(b)'s lattice (Tl, Ca, Ba, Cu, O)
and the particles of 34.17's pairs and 34.4's neutrinos: `F.el('e-')`,
`F.el('e+')`, `F.el('p+')`, `F.el('gamma')`, `F.el('nu')`. Through `F.fact`: the
red and blue shift of a galaxy's two sides in 34.18(a), a star's glow; the WMAP
sky map stays a photograph.

## Referents

Each section lists its own when it is built. Planned:

- **34.1.** The home galaxy and a distant one of the Hubble figure, where the
  text names "our galaxy" against the receding others.
- **34.2.** The elevator and the flashlight beam (34.10), the Sun and the star
  whose light it bends (34.11), the lensing galaxy and the quasar behind it
  (34.12), the black hole and its companion star (34.13).
- **34.4.** The galaxy's approaching and receding sides (34.18(a)); the
  observed and the predicted rotation curve of 34.18(b), one curve per
  referent.
- **34.5.** The two pendulums of a chaos figure, if drawn, whose nearly equal
  starts the text compares.
- **34.6.** Mercury's curve (34.23) is a single curve and keeps `resistivity`;
  the three trials of one sample in 34.25(a), which the text does not name one
  by one, take `F.cat(i)` with a label, offset by the figure's referent count.

Where a subscript names a referent (the velocity $v_\text{rec}$ of the receding
side against $v_\text{app}$), the main letter wears the category and the
subscript the referent, and its variables row names it in `ref`.

The frame of a figure and its labels stay in ink, and with colour coding off
every figure stays legible from its labels.
