## What the page is

The chapter's own introduction, kept as a page of its own and listed before
9.1, as rule 21 asks. It is the book's two opening paragraphs with the boxed
note that sits between them: the first paragraph asks what desks, bridges,
buildings, trees and mountains have in common, answers that they are
motionless relative to the Earth and so have no acceleration, points out
that a car at constant velocity has none either, and concludes from
Newton's second law that the net external force on all of them is zero, so
that they are in equilibrium; the boxed note names statics as the study of
forces in equilibrium and says what the chapter will add to it; the second
paragraph says that there are two conditions for equilibrium and that they
are the topics of the first two sections. The book prints no learning
objectives, no summary, no glossary entry and no exercises here, so the page
carries none of them, and it has no lead, since nothing is written in the
book's place.
