## Symbols

Never change an existing symbol row (rule of the job). Rows the chapter would
otherwise have wanted are already taken and are **not** reused for this
chapter's meanings:

- `B` is Chapter 3's **vector B** with `\kB`, a position, and `B_x` and `B_y`
  its components; `B_bulk` is Chapter 5's **bulk modulus**, also written $B$.
  The magnetic field is `B_mag` (LaTeX `B`, type `magnetic-field`, macro
  `\kBmag`), and 22.10's field of the first wire is `B_1mag` (`B_1`,
  `\kBmagone`).
- `I` is Chapter 10's **moment of inertia** with `\kI`, and `I_1` and `I_2` are
  Chapter 17's **intensities**. The current is Chapter 20's `I_curr` (`\kIcur`)
  and the two currents of 22.10 are Chapter 21's `I_onecurr` (`\kIcurone`) and
  `I_twocurr` (`\kIcurtwo`); all three landed in this wave and are used by
  name, never restaged.
- `ε_0` is Chapter 19's **permittivity** and Chapter 21 writes the emf as
  `emf`, LaTeX `\mathcal{E}`, a voltage with `\kemf`. The Hall emf of 22.6 is
  written `\varepsilon` where the book writes it, so this chapter stages
  `emf_hall` (LaTeX `\varepsilon`, type `voltage`, macro `\kemfhall`) rather
  than changing Chapter 21's row or the book's glyph.
- `w` is Chapter 4's **weight** with `\kwgt`. The width of 22.8's loop is
  `w_loop` (LaTeX `w`, untyped, no macro), a length and so in ink.
- `N` is Chapter 4's **normal force** with `\kN`. The number of turns is
  Chapter 13's untyped `N_count` (LaTeX `N`) and is used as it stands, as is
  the untyped `n` for the turns per unit length.
- `R` is Chapter 6's **circumference-and-radius** row, a position with `\kR`,
  and is the radius of 22.9's loop as it stands; `r_curv` (LaTeX `r`, position,
  `\kr`) is the radius of a charge's circle in 22.5 and the shortest distance
  to a wire in 22.9 and 22.10, as Chapter 6 keyed it.
- `E` is Chapter 7's **total energy** with `\kE`; the electric field is Chapter
  18's `E_field` (`\kEf`) and is used by name in 22.6 and 22.11.
- `l`, `A`, `θ`, `m`, `v`, `q`, `F`, `τ`, `m_e` and `q_e` are used as they
  stand. The magnetic force is Chapter 4's `F`, since a magnetic force is a
  force and takes that hue; the torque on a loop is Chapter 9's `τ`.
