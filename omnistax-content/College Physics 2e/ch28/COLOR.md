# Chapter 28 colour plan

Prepared 2026-09-28 in the chapter's prep pass, beside `config.md`, and brought
under root `RULES.md` item 7 on 2026-10-04. The chapter declares no type and
hard-codes no hue except the one physical-fact colour below.

Relativity compares two observers' numbers for one quantity, so the rule that a
variant of a category keeps its hue does most of the work here: proper time and
dilated time are one `time` hue, proper and contracted length one `position`
hue, $u$ and $u'$ one `velocity` hue. The proper value is the variant with the
subscript 0 and is drawn hollow or dashed, as an initial value is in this book;
the two frames are told apart by their labels and by the referents at rest in
them, never by a second category hue.

| Quantity | Category | Treatment |
|---|---|---|
| $c$, $v$, $u$, $u'$ | `velocity` | one hue; the wall at $c$ is a dashed line in that hue |
| $\Delta t$, $\Delta t_0$ | `time` | $\Delta t_0$ hollow or dashed |
| $L$, $L_0$, $D$, $s$, $\lambda_{\text{s}}$, $\lambda_{\text{obs}}$ | `position` | $L_0$ hollow or dashed |
| $f_{\text{s}}$, $f_{\text{obs}}$ | `frequency` | 28.4 only |
| $p$ | `momentum` | 28.5 and 28.6 |
| $E$, $E_0$, $\text{KE}_{\text{rel}}$, $\text{KE}_{\text{class}}$, $W_{\text{net}}$ | `energy` | $\text{KE}_{\text{class}}$ dashed beside the relativistic curve |
| $m$, $\Delta m$ | `mass` | the rest mass, in the text and the readouts of 28.5 and 28.6 |
| the electron's field lines | `electric-field` | 28.3's Figure 28.12 |
| the angle at the base of the energy triangle | `angle` | 28.6's Sim |
| $q$, $I$, $t$, $V$ of the car battery | `charge`, `current`, `time`, `voltage` | Example 28.7 only |
| $\gamma$, $v/c$ | none | ink |

The subscripts of $\lambda_{\text{s}}$ and $f_{\text{s}}$ wear the colour of
28.4's Doppler source, and those of $\lambda_{\text{obs}}$ and $f_{\text{obs}}$
the colour of the Earth that receives the waves.

through `F.fact` while the wavelength is visible and is ink outside it.

Conventions: the electron of 28.3's Figure 28.12 is drawn with `F.el('e-')`, the
one use of the element palette in the chapter; a muon, an electron or a π-meson
the text merely names is told by its label.
