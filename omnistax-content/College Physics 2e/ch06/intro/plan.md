## What the page is

The chapter's own introduction, kept as a page of its own and listed before
6.1, as rule 21 asks. It is the book's two opening paragraphs under the
chapter opener's photograph: the first says that most motion is curved and
that Newton's first law means there must be forces causing it, so the
chapter will study the curves and the forces together; the second names
uniform circular motion as the simplest form of curved motion and
distinguishes rotational from translational motion. The book prints no
learning objectives, no summary and no exercises here, so the page carries
none of them, and it has no lead, since nothing is written in the book's
place.
