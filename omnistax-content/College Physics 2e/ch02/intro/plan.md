## What the page is

The chapter's own introduction, kept as a page of its own and listed
before 2.1, as rule 21 asks. It is the book's two opening paragraphs on
motion and on what kinematics is, under the chapter opener's photograph.

has no lead, since nothing is written in the book's place. The one
defined term, kinematics, is already in the chapter's glossary under 2.1
