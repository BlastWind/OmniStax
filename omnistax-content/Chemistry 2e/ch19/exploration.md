Written 2026-10-05, before the chapter was prepared. The four modules were converted with `python3 tools/convert.py 19` and read in full. ## Why this chapter

Chapter 18 walked the representative elements; Chapter 19 turns to the d- and f-blocks. 19.1 defines the transition and inner transition metals and their series, reads their reactivity from reduction potentials and their many oxidation states from their configurations, isolates iron (blast furnace, steel), copper (smelting, blister copper) and silver (hydrometallurgy), and surveys their halides, oxides, hydroxides, carbonates and salts, with a How Sciences Interconnect box on high-temperature superconductors. 19.2 builds coordination chemistry on the Lewis acid-base picture of 15.2: central metal, ligands, donor atoms, coordination sphere and number, mono- and polydentate ligands and chelates, Werner's naming rules, the geometries that coordination numbers give, geometric, optical, linkage and ionization isomers, and complexes in nature, industry and medicine. 19.3 explains colour and magnetism with crystal field theory: the d orbitals split by six ligands on the axes into e<sub>g</sub> and t<sub>2g</sub>, Δ<sub>oct</sub> set by the spectrochemical series, its contest with the pairing energy P giving high- and low-spin complexes, the tetrahedral and square planar patterns, magnetic moments, and the complementary colour a complex shows when it absorbs Δ<sub>oct</sub>.

## BE INSPIRING

Make the colours of the opener's minerals and of Figure 19.12's six solutions something the reader can make. Bring six ligands in along the axes of a free ion and watch the two d orbitals that point at them climb away from the three that point between them; swap iodide for cyanide along the spectrochemical series and see the gap widen until the electrons give up their separate rooms and pair, the magnet losing its pull, the absorbed light moving from red to violet and the solution's colour swinging to its complement. Turn [Co(en)₃]³⁺ beside its mirror image and try, and fail, to lay one on the other; flip cisplatin to transplatin and see the dipole vanish. Ride a lump of ore down a blast furnace through its temperature zones as carbon monoxide strips its oxygen, and cool YBa₂Cu₃O₇ through 92 K until its resistance is gone.
