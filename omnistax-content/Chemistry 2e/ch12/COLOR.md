# Chapter 12 colour plan

Prepared 2026-09-28 with `config.md` and brought under root `RULES.md` item 7
on 2026-10-04, when only the introduction was built. Colour reaches these pages
in item 7's four ways, fact, convention, referent and category, and where two
apply the earlier wins. This file records what is particular to the chapter;
it invents no hue.

## Categories

| Quantity | Type | Treatment |
|---|---|---|
| [A], [A]₀, [A]ₜ, [B], Δ[A], Δ[B], a species' concentration | `concentration` | The concentration axis and each readout; the flask shading of 12.12 as an opacity of this hue. ln[A] and 1/[A] are functions of it and stay ink, axes included |
| The rate of a reaction, an instantaneous, average or initial rate | `rate` | Every tangent or secant slope read as a rate. A rate is never a concentration |
| k, k₁, k₂, k₋₁, the frequency factor A | `rate-constant` | The slope of an integrated-rate-law plot read as −k; A is its high-temperature limit |
| t, Δt, t₁/₂ | `time` | |
| E_a, ΔH, the energy of a barrier, the energy axis of a reaction diagram and of the distribution | `energy` | An activation energy is an energy |
| T, T₁, T₂ | `temperature` | 12.2's collision box, 12.5 |
| The amount of a reactant in a collision box, if a slider reads moles | `amount` | |

These stay in ink: the orders m and n, R, ln k, 1/T, the stoichiometric
coefficients, a percent decomposed, counts of collisions.

A word or phrase wears a category when it names a particular one the reader can
point at: the H₂O₂ concentration falling from 1.000 M to 0.500 M in the first
half-life, the half-life from 6.00 hours to 12.00 hours, the E_a of 180 kJ
mol⁻¹ found in Example 12.13. The kind in general ("the half-life of a
reaction", "the rate of a reaction" as a definition), its unit and its
definition stay ink.

## Facts and conventions

`F.el` for every atom and molecule: CO, O₂, CO₂ (12.13), NO₂, NO, CO, CO₂
(12.17), H₂, C₂H₄, C₂H₆ and the nickel surface (12.23), O₃ and Cl in an ozone
cycle; a collision box draws an identified pair (O + CO, or NO + O₃), never an
anonymous A + BC. Through `F.fact`: iodine's brown on a test strip and the
colours of the urinalysis strip, if drawn. H₂O₂ is colourless, so 12.12's
shading is concentration, not fact.

## Referents

Each section lists its own when it is built. Planned:

- **12.1.** The species of a reaction on one concentration–time graph (H₂O₂,
  H₂O and O₂; NH₃, N₂ and H₂ of 12.5), one curve per species. A
  concentration written [H₂O₂] keeps its brackets in `concentration` and the
  formula in the species' colour, its variables row naming the species in
  `ref`.
- **12.2.** The tungsten and quartz curves of 12.11; the hot and cold boxes of
  a temperature Sim.
- **12.3.** The trials of an initial-rates table, where the text names trial 1
  against trial 2.
- **12.4.** The three flasks of 12.12 at successive half-lives, if the text
  names them one by one; otherwise one flask over a clock.
- **12.5.** The two temperatures T₁ and T₂ of 12.15(b), one distribution curve
  each, the subscripts in the referents' colours; the two activation energies
  of 12.15(a).
- **12.6.** Each step of a mechanism on its reaction diagram, where the text
  names step 1 and step 2; k₁ and k₂ split, the 1 and 2 in the steps' colours.
- **12.7.** The uncatalyzed and catalyzed paths of 12.19 and Example 12.15's
  diagrams, one curve each, with the E_a brackets in `energy`; the enzyme and
  the substrate of 12.25.

The frame of a figure and its labels stay in ink, and with colour coding off
every figure stays legible from its labels.
