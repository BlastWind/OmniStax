# Chapter 12 colour plan

Prepared 2026-09-28 with `config.md`, brought under root `RULES.md` item 7
on 2026-10-04, when only the introduction was built, and set to the bindings as
built by the chapter pass of 2026-10-05. Colour reaches these pages
in item 7's four ways, fact, convention, referent and category, and where two
apply the earlier wins. This file records what is particular to the chapter;
it invents no hue.

## Categories

| Quantity | Type | Treatment |
|---|---|---|
| [A], [A]₀, [A]ₜ, [B], Δ[A], Δ[B], a species' concentration | `concentration` | The concentration axis and each readout; the flask shading of 12.12 as an opacity of this hue. ln[A] and 1/[A] are functions of it and stay ink, axes included |
| The rate of a reaction, an instantaneous, average or initial rate | `rate` | Every tangent or secant slope read as a rate. A rate is never a concentration |
| k, k₁, k₂, k₋₁, the frequency factor A | `rate-constant` | Typed wherever it is written: `\kk`, `\kkone`, `\kktwo`, `\kkmone` and `\kAfreq` in prose, forms and readouts, and the chord of 12.4's straight plot read as k; A is its high-temperature limit. The chapter notes' untyped k was set aside, since `book.json` declares the type and binds k to it |
| t, Δt, t₁/₂ | `time` | |
| E_a, ΔH, the energy of a barrier, the energy axis of a reaction diagram and of the distribution | `energy` | An activation energy is an energy |
| T, T₁, T₂ | `temperature` | 12.2's collision box, 12.5's distribution and plot, the 298 K of 12.7's readout |

These stay in ink: the orders m and n, R, ln k, 1/T, the stoichiometric
coefficients, a percent decomposed, counts of collisions.

A word or phrase wears a category when it names a concept of that category,
in general or in particular, as root rule 7 has said since it was changed:
"the rate of a reaction" in a definition as much as the H₂O₂ concentration
falling from 1.000 M to 0.500 M, "the half-life of a reaction" as much as the
half-life from 6.00 hours to 12.00 hours. A word in another sense ("at the same
time", "a range of values") stays ink, and a value no concept
names ("1.8 × 10⁵ J mol⁻¹") is marked by its type. 12.2's collision box binds
`time` as well as `temperature`, `concentration` and `rate`, for Δt and its
strip's time axis.

## Facts and conventions

`F.el` for every atom and molecule drawn: NO, O₃, NO₂ and O₂ (12.2's box),
CO, O₂, CO₂ and O (12.13), cyclobutane and ethylene in 3D (12.6), NO₂, CO, NO
and CO₂ (12.17), H₂, C₂H₄, C₂H₆ and the nickel surface (12.23). A collision
draws an identified pair, never an anonymous A + BC: NO + O₃ in 12.2's
many-collisions box, CO + O₂ in 12.5's single oriented collision. No figure
paints a fact: the test strips and the ozone map are photographs, and H₂O₂ is
colorless, so 12.12's shading is concentration.
