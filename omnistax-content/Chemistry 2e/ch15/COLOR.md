# Chapter 15 colour plan

Prepared 2026-09-28 with `config.md`. It refines the book's `COLOR.md` for what this chapter draws; root rules 7 and 22 hold. The chapter's central quantity is concentration: every ion concentration, a molar solubility, pH and pOH are one type and every log concentration axis wears its hue. K_sp, Q_sp, K_f, K_d and the net K are equilibrium constants, untyped by the book's decision, and are drawn in ink, even as the K_sp line on a concentration plane (the line is ink, the axes are concentration).

## Atoms, curves and physical colour

- Ions and molecules take `F.el` everywhere (Ag⁺, Cl⁻, Ca²⁺, F⁻, water, NH₃, complex ions), the ion keeping its element colour and a charge mark.
- Two salts on one plane (selective precipitation) or two species curves (Al³⁺ and Al(OH)₄⁻): `F.cat(i)` per salt or species; never the concentration hue.
- A precipitate's own colour (AgCl white, AgBr cream, PbCrO₄ yellow, Ag₂CrO₄ red) is a physical fact, a named constant in the plan line, unchanged in both themes.
- The donated electron pair in a Lewis equation is marked by a symbolic arrow in ink.

## What stays in ink

K_sp, Q_sp, K_f, K_d, K, K_a2, x, p and q, the K_sp line and every log term. Macros used: `\kconcMion`, `\kconcXion`, `\kconcHyd`, `\kconcOH`, `\kpH`, `\kpOH`, `\kM`, `\km`.
