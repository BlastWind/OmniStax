# Chapter 3 colour plan

Refines the book's `COLOR.md`; no new hue.

## Facts and conventions

None. The salt in the tanks of 3.1 is drawn as a shade of the tank's own referent colour whose depth is the concentration x/V, not as a fact colour; no figure draws a real colour.

## Categories

- `function`: a component x₁(t), x₂(t), y₁, u₁, a scalar solution ξ_k(t), the amounts of salt x₁, x₂ in grams, a displacement x₁ of a cart, the forcing g_k(t).
- `derivative`: x₁′, y₂′, x⃗′ written as a derivative, x⃗″; the velocity of a cart.
- `vector`: x⃗ and x⃗(t) where the section treats a solution as one vector, an eigenvector v⃗, a generalized eigenvector (drawn hollow), f⃗, F⃗, b⃗, c⃗ when it is a solved-for vector of coefficients (3.6, 3.9), the columns of a matrix, the displacement vector of 3.6.
- `matrix`: A, P, P(t), M, K, X(t), E, D, e^{tA}, A⁻¹, Aᵀ, the identity I.
- `eigenvalue`: λ, λ_k, a ± ib, α of 3.6 (α² an eigenvalue), −ω², the multiplicities' eigenvalue.
- `frequency`: the natural frequencies ω_i of 3.6, the forcing ω.
- `region`: the parallelogram, image of the unit square under [a b; c d], in 3.2's `sim-determinant-area` (a book type, no new hue).
- `vector-field`: the direction field of 3.1 and 3.5.
- `curve`: a trajectory (solution curve) in the phase plane.
- `point`: an initial point (x₀, y₀) and the critical point at the origin.
- `integral`: the integral of the integrating-factor and variation-of-parameters solutions (3.9).
- `series`: the Taylor series of e^{at} and of e^{A} (3.8).
- `error`: the gap between a truncated series and e^{tA} (3.8).

Untyped, in ink: t, x as independent variables; the arbitrary constants c₁, C₁, a_i, b_i, α₁, α₂ and the constant vector c⃗ of a general solution; the matrix entries a, b, c, d, a_ij as numbers; r, V, s of the tanks; m, k, m_i, k_i of the carts; n, k, i, j as indices; det, the determinant's value (a number); the scalar α of 3.5; h of 3.2's exercises.
