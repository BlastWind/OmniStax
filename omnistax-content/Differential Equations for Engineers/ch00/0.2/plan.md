## Conventions for later sections (the template)

1. Ids: subsections by a short slug of the book's title; a paragraph that introduces concepts `p#<slug>`; a numbered display `div.equation#eq-<n>` (`eq-2` in 0.3; in Chapter 1, `eq-1-3` for (1.3)) and a cited one linked `<a href="#eq-<n>">(n)</a>`; a boxed or form display `div.equation#eq-<form slug>` (the form's `anchor` is `N.M-eq-<form slug>`); Example N.M.k `div.example#ex-N-M-k` with `<h3>Example N.M.k</h3>` (and the book's name after " · " where it gives one); figures `sim-<idea>`, faithful copies `fig-<idea>`. A paragraph that only continues the prose keeps no id unless an exercise follows it or a row cites it.
2. Exercises: id `e<k>` for Exercise N.M.k (`e101` for N.M.101), `source_id` and `source_number` the printed number; inline Try Its `place: {at: inline, after: <span>}` with `<div class="exercises" data-place="<span>">` right after that span, the span being the display or paragraph the book prints just before the exercise; every end exercise `place: {at: end}` with a `cite`; `exercise_concepts` rows for every exercise.
3. Defined terms: `<em><span data-concept="…">…</span></em>` at the definition, plain `data-concept` spans at later mentions in the same sense; a term the book indexes without italics (`four fundamental equations`) a plain span.
4. Figure and equation numbers as printed; widths in pixels at 96 per inch from the converter's `width:` (4.5in → 432) in `widths` and `data-original-width`; a numbered book plot is `kind: sim` with `number`, `originals`, `original_caption` as plain text (no math in an attribute), eyebrow "Figure N"; an unnumbered book image copied faithfully is `kind: figure` with `originals` and no number, eyebrow "Figure", and carries neither `data-figure` nor `data-original-caption`; a Sim that replaces nothing has no `originals`, eyebrow "Sim"; every row lists `draws` and its own `ai` (`part: "built"`). Chapter 0's "Figure N" citations are linked by hand; from Chapter 1 the app links "Figure N.M".
5. Unkeyed answers: AI-marked (`generated_by: ai`), the answer and the reasoning in the book's terms, computed values checked with SymPy first; the book's `\exsol` as `solution` with `generated_by: source`; shapes `choice` (yes/no, true/false), `number` (one value, with `unit`), `multi` (several named values, parts named by the book's letters or symbols), `open` otherwise; one card per `tasks` list with `<ol type="a">` in the prompt and a)–d) in the solution.
6. Coverage: one `introduces` row per concept on the `p#` (or `div#ex-…`) where the book defines it; `uses` rows on every paragraph, display, figure or example that leans on a concept introduced elsewhere; only this book's concept ids.
7. Symbols: the section's own through the `\k` macros in text, captions, headlines, readouts, prompts and solutions; one-off letters of an exercise, and a section symbol the book turns to another role, stay plain.
8. Section fields: `exercises_lead` the book's line above or inside the set; `exercise_notes` where the answers come from; `notes` the dropped section notes in one sentence; `ai` the maker per part; `built` the ISO date; `objectives` and `summary_html` empty (the book prints neither).
9. Calculus: a link on the book's own words for what the page assumes (`/calculus/chNN/N.M/`), never a repeat of it.
10. Figures: still unless the idea has a clock; the book's numbers the defaults; a choice for a discrete state, a slider for a quantity, detents at the book's values; every readout true as written in every state, written through the macros; labels on panels beside their thing, hover names where labels would crowd; no hex colours, no painted text.
