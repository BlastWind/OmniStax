# Chapter 0 colour

Refines the book's `COLOR.md`; no new hue.

- Categories: `function` for the dependent variable and every solution (x, y, P, θ, u and the curves drawn of them); `derivative` for every derivative symbol (dx/dt, y′, y″, the partials of 0.3), which in 0.3 makes the order and the linearity of an equation readable at a glance; `point` for an initial condition (the hollow dot at t = 0 in Figure 1, the two measured points of Figure 2).
- Ink: t and x as independent variables, the constants C, C₁, C₂, D₁, D₂, k, A, L, the coefficients aₙ(x) and the right-hand sides b(x), f(t) of 0.3, and Maxwell's fields, which 0.3 only names.
- Facts and conventions: none.
- Referents: in 0.3's pendulum Sim the linear and the nonlinear solution are two referents (`linear-pendulum`, `nonlinear-pendulum`), each one curve in its own colour, and the text marks the two solutions where it names them. The arch Sim of 0.2 draws the catenary in `function` and the comparison parabola dashed in ink, since the text names no particular parabola.
- Exercises colour only the symbols the section's variables list; their one-off letters (A, H, φ, p, T, s, u, v, F) stay in ink.
