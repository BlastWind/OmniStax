Chapter 10
Trapped ions: quantum state
engineering
Although our discussion has concentrated on interactions between photons
and neutral atoms, similar techniques and physical processes arise with
trapped ions. Unlike neutral atoms, charged ions can be confined in traps
so tight that the quantization of motional states becomes very important.
This leads to rather different processes which bear a remarkable similarity
to the dynamics of atoms and photons in cavity quantum electrodynamics.
