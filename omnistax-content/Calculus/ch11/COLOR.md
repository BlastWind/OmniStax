## What the chapter colours

No convention. One fact: Figure 11.1, Figure 11.50 and the original of Figure 11.36 are photographs in their own colours. The categories:

- `curve`, the chapter's main type: a parametric curve C and its trace, the cycloid, the hypocycloid, the witch of Agnesi, the curtate and prolate cycloids, a polar curve r = f(θ) and its named families (the line through the pole, the circle, the spirals, the cardioid, the limaçon, the rose), a conic section (parabola, ellipse, hyperbola) and its branches. Its orientation marks wear the curve's colour.
- `angle`: θ of a polar point and of a polar curve, the angle of the ray a figure turns, the angle t a wheel has turned through, the angle of rotation of axes, α and β only where a figure marks them as rays.
- `point`: the moving point (x(t), y(t)) or (r, θ), a critical point, the vertex of a parabola, a focus, the vertices of an ellipse or hyperbola, the pole, a cusp, the points A, B, P of a construction.
- `line`: a tangent line, the polar axis and the rays θ = K, a directrix, the asymptotes of a hyperbola, the major and minor axes, the rotated axes x′ and y′.
- `derivative`: dy/dx, d²y/dx², x′(t), y′(t), dy/dt, dx/dt, the slope a tangent carries, the speed s′(t) of the baseball.
- `integral`: the area A under a parametric curve and its rectangles, the arc lengths s and L and their chords, the surface area S and its band, a polar area A and its sectors.
- `surface`: the cone and its nappes (11.5), the surface swept by revolving a parametric curve (11.2).
- `region`: the polar region whose area 11.4 takes, where it is drawn as a set rather than as the area's value.
- `function`: f in r = f(θ) and y = F(x) where the text names the function.

In ink: x, y, t, r, z as variables and coordinates; the constants a, b, c, h, k, p, e, the coefficients A–F of a general second-degree equation, K, n, k; the distances d of the distance formula; the radius of a wheel; Earth's day number.
