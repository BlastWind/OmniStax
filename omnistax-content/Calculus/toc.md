# Calculus (OpenStax) table of contents

The three volumes of OpenStax Calculus (collections `calculus-volume-1`, `-2` and `-3` of the CNXML bundle) merged into one book of 17 chapters. Volume 2 opens on the last two chapters of Volume 1 and Volume 3 on the last chapter of Volume 2, the same modules, so each chapter is kept once, where it first appears. Each line gives the merged number, the title, the module, and where the volumes print it. Chapters 1–6 are Volume 1's, 7–11 are Volume 2 Chapters 3–7, 12–17 are Volume 3 Chapters 2–7. Inside a chapter the section, example, checkpoint, theorem, figure and exercise counts are the volume's, so only the chapter part of a printed number changes (Volume 2's Example 3.4 is Example 7.4 here). Each section module ends with its own Key Concepts, Key Equations where it has any, glossary and Section Exercises; the last module of each chapter also carries the Chapter Review Exercises. Exercises are numbered chapter-wide; the ranges are in `modules.json`.
