# Chapter 12 colour plan

Prepared 2026-10-13 with `config.md`. It refines the book's `COLOR.md` for what this chapter draws; root rules 7 and 22 hold, and nothing here invents a hue. The types are the 18 of `book.json`.

## What the chapter colours

No convention. Facts: the photographs (12.1, 12.73, 12.85, 12.86, 12.105) in their own colours. The categories:

- `vector`, the chapter's main type: u, v, w, a, b, c, p, q, r, s, F (a force), n (a normal), i, j, k, the zero vector, PQ⃗ and every vector named by its points, kv, v + w, v − w, a unit vector, a resultant, a projection proj_u v, a cross product u × v, a direction vector, torque τ, a position vector r₀. A vector's arrow, its name and its symbol wear one hue; its components laid along the axes wear it faintly.
- `point`: an initial and a terminal point, P, Q, R, M and every named point in space, a centre of a sphere, the point a line or plane passes through.
- `plane`: the coordinate planes, a plane x = c, a plane and its vector or scalar equation, the plane of intersection's two planes.
- `line`: a line in space, its equations, skew lines, a line of intersection, rulings of a cylinder.
- `surface`: a sphere, a cylinder, the quadric surfaces, a coordinate surface r = c, ρ = c, φ = c.
- `curve`: a trace of a surface, a circle in space (12.31's linked circles).
- `angle`: θ between two vectors or two planes, the direction angles α, β, γ, the polar angle θ of cylindrical and spherical coordinates, φ, a latitude or longitude.
- `matrix`: a 2 × 2 or 3 × 3 determinant and its array, a minor.
- `region`: an octant, a parallelepiped, the prism of the distance formula where it is drawn as a solid.

In ink: x, y, z, t and coordinates; a scalar k, c, r, s; a magnitude ‖v‖, a dot product, a scalar projection comp_u v, a triple scalar product, work W, a distance d, a volume V and an area (scalars); ρ, r and z as coordinates.

## Rules the sections keep

- Two vectors of one figure that the text names apart (v and w, u and v) wear `vector` both, told apart by their names, unless a figure's lesson is the pair (the referents above).
- A cross product u × v wears `vector`; the parallelogram it measures wears `region` faintly; a dot product's number stays ink.
- A trace wears `curve` on the surface it lies on; the slicing plane wears `plane`, translucent.
