# Chapter 14 colour plan

Prepared 2026-10-13 with `config.md`. It refines the book's `COLOR.md` for what this chapter draws; root rules 7 and 22 hold, and nothing here invents a hue. The types are the 18 of `book.json`.

## What the chapter colours

No convention. Facts: the photographs (14.1, 14.7, 14.25) and the book's images 14.12 and 14.26 in their own colours. The categories:

- `function`: f(x, y), g(x, y), f(x, y, z), w, a profit or objective function, the solution u of a partial differential equation, the one-variable stand-in of 14.30.
- `surface`, the chapter's main picture: the graph z = f(x, y) of every function of two variables, a hemisphere, a paraboloid, a saddle; a level surface f(x, y, z) = c.
- `curve`: a level curve f(x, y) = c and the contour map they make, a vertical trace, the ellipse an implicit equation draws, the boundary of a domain where it is parameterized.
- `derivative`: f_x, f_y, f_z, ∂f/∂x and every Leibniz form, f_xx, f_xy, f_yx, f_yy, the directional derivative D_u f; a slope read off a trace.
- `vector`: the gradient ∇f, the unit direction u, the normal f_x i + f_y j − k, a tangent vector to a level curve, ∇g of a constraint.
- `plane`: the tangent plane and the linear approximation L(x, y) whose graph it is, translucent; a slicing plane z = c, x = a or y = b.
- `line`: a tangent line to a trace or a curve on the surface, a secant line, the constraint lines 20x + 4y = 216 and x + 2y = 7.
- `point`: P₀, (a, b), an interior or boundary point, a critical point, a saddle point, a local or absolute extremum.
- `set`: a domain D and a range, a δ disk and a δ ball, an open, closed or connected set, a bounded set.
- `region`: a region (open, connected, nonempty), the rectangle and disk of 14.7's absolute extrema where drawn as the domain.
- `limit`: the limit L and the ε slab about it.
- `error`: the error term E(x, y) and the gap between a surface and its tangent plane.

In ink: x, y, z, t, u, v as variables, a, b, x₀, y₀, z₀, h, k, Δx, Δy, dx, dy, the total differential dz and the change Δz (numbers), the discriminant D, λ, c, ε and δ (drawn as bands in the colour of what they bound).

## Rules the sections keep

- A function's symbol f and its graph's sheet are one hue apart: f wears `function` in the text and the readout, the sheet it draws wears `surface`, so the reader sees "the graph of f" as a thing distinct from f. Level curves on the floor and on the sheet wear `curve`.
- Two gradients of one figure (∇f and ∇g in 14.8) both wear `vector`, told apart by their names, as Chapter 12 tells two vectors apart.
- The objective's level curves and the constraint of 14.8 are told apart by type: level curves `curve`, a linear constraint `line`.
