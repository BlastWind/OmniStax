# Chapter 6 colour plan

Prepared 2026-10-12 with `config.md`. It refines the book's `COLOR.md` for what this chapter draws; root rules 7 and 22 hold, and nothing here invents a hue. The types are the 18 of `book.json`.

## What the chapter colours

No convention. Facts: the four photographs (Figure 6.1, the dam photo of Example 6.28, Figures 6.72 and 6.83) are their own colours; the water of 6.5's tanks, trough and dam is drawn in a type's or ink's tint, not a fact colour, since its colour teaches nothing. The categories:

- `integral`: every quantity the chapter computes by an integral and the pieces that sum to it: an area between curves and its rectangles, a volume and its disks, washers and shells, an arc length and its segments' lengths, a surface area and its frustum bands, a mass, a work, a hydrostatic force, a moment Mₓ, M_y, M; A, V, V_shell, W, S (6.4), F (6.5's hydrostatic force) and m where it is a total mass.
- `region`: the region R or Q revolved or integrated over, a cross-section, a cylinder, a lamina, a frustum as a solid, and the solid of revolution itself: the solid wears the region's hue, since it is the region swept.
- `surface`: a surface of revolution (6.4), drawn as a sheet without the region inside it.
- `function`: f, g, u(y), v(y) and every curve that is the graph of one; a density ρ(x), a variable force F(x), the depth s(x) and width w(x) of a plate; ln x, eˣ, aˣ; a growth or decay model y = y₀e^{kt}, a temperature T(t); the hyperbolic functions and a catenary.
- `derivative`: f′ and g′ inside the arc-length and surface-area integrands, y′ = ky and T′, the derivatives of the hyperbolic functions and their inverses.
- `point`: the center of mass x̄ (and ȳ) and a centroid, drawn as the balance point.
- `line`: an axis of revolution other than a coordinate axis (y = −2, x = −1, x = 4), drawn dashed, and the line of symmetry of 6.6.

In ink: x, y, t, the endpoints a, b, c, d, Δx and Δy, xᵢ*, n; a radius r, a height h, a slant height s or l, the spring constant k, a growth or decay constant k, an initial state y₀; the point masses m₁, m₂, … and their positions; the constant density ρ of a lamina (6.6) and the weight density of water; the number e and the constant of integration C.

## Rules the sections keep

- The region wears `region`; the integral that measures it wears `integral`, and so do the rectangles, disks, washers, shells and bands whose sum approximates it, as Chapter 5's Riemann rectangles do. The solid of revolution is the region swept, so it wears `region`; the slice inside it wears `integral`.
- Two curves bounding one region are referents of the figure (Chapter 1's ruling 4): each curve in its referent colour, the letters f, g, u, v in `function`.
- An axis of revolution that is a coordinate axis is ink; one that is another line wears `line`, dashed.
- In 6.6 a center of mass is a `point`, a filled dot with a fulcrum or pin under it; the moments that locate it are `integral`.
