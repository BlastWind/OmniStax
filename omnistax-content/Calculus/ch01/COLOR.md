# Chapter 1 colour plan

Prepared 2026-10-12 with `config.md`. It refines the book's `COLOR.md` for what this chapter draws; root rules 7 and 22 hold, and nothing here invents a hue.

## What the chapter colours

`function` is the chapter's category (config 21): every function symbol the text names (f, g, s(t), f⁻¹), every named class of function (a linear, polynomial, rational, trigonometric, exponential, logarithmic, hyperbolic function and their inverses) where a mention names it, and every curve that is the graph of one. Four more run through the chapter (the ruling of 2026-10-12): `set` for a domain, a range and an interval, the symbols D and R, and a written interval or set as a value (`data-type="set"`), drawn as the ovals of a map and the bands along an axis; `point` for a zero, an intercept and a marked point (x, f(x)), drawn as the dots on a graph and the point a reader drags; `line` for the forms of a line's equation (1.2), the lines of the vertical and horizontal line tests and an asymptote; `angle` for θ and the radian (1.3). Two others appear a few times: `derivative` for the slope m of a line (1.2) and the slope of the tangent to eˣ at 0 (1.5, Figure 1.45), the tangent itself a `line`; `limit` for the value a function approaches at infinity (1.2, "Behavior at Infinity"), its horizontal asymptote a `line`. No fact and no convention: the opener and the two photographs keep their own colours.

## Rules the sections keep

- In ink: the variables x, y, t; the numbers a, b, c, h and the coordinates, b of the intercept (0, b) among them; the degree n and the coefficients aₙ, …, a₀; the parameters A, B, C, α of a transformed sine; the period p; the constants e and π.
- In `set`: D, R, and the letters A, B, D, E of the composite's definition in 1.1; a written interval or set that is the value of a domain, a range or an interval of increase is marked `data-type="set"`, a working step that names a set on the way to it stays ink.
- In `point`: a written intercept or marked point, `data-type="point"`; a general pair (x, y) stays ink. The 24 readings of Table 1.1 are the function's graph and wear `function`; the point dragged along it is a `point`.
- A relation that is not a function (the sideways curve of Figure 1.8(b)) is drawn in ink: it is not a value of the category; the points where a line meets it are still points.
- A referent's curve wears its referent colour, a single curve its category's; f⁻¹ beside f is a referent of the graph, not a variant. A map's ovals are sets and wear `set`; its arrows wear their referents' colours.
- A function written only as a value ("f(5) = 16") is ink; the function symbol in it wears `function`.
