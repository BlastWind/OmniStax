# Chapter 2 colour plan

Prepared 2026-10-12 with `config.md`. It refines the book's `COLOR.md` for what this chapter draws; root rules 7 and 22 hold, and nothing here invents a hue. The types are the 18 of `book.json`.

## What the chapter colours

No fact and no convention: the opener and Figure 2.22 are photographs in their own colours. The categories:

- `function`: every function the text names (f, g, h, k, s(t), p and q, and the ratio m/m₀ of Einstein's equation graphed against v/c), and every curve that is the graph of one.
- `limit`: L and M as limits, the dashed level a limit sits on, a hole's height, the ε-band L ± ε, the words that name a limit, one-sided or infinite, the intuitive and the ε–δ definitions.
- `derivative`: the tangent line, a rate of change, m_sec as a slope, average and instantaneous velocity (the applied quantities wear the type of the slope they are).
- `line`: a secant line, a vertical asymptote x = a (drawn dashed).
- `integral`: the area under a curve and the rectangles that approximate it (2.1 only).
- `angle`: θ and its arc on the unit circle (2.3), as Chapter 1 types it.

In ink: x, t, the point a and the number x approaches; c and K as constants; n and k; the z and c of the IVT; ε and δ as symbols (their bands take the colour of what they bound, the ε-band `limit`, the δ-band ink, as the book's `COLOR.md` says); M where it is a bound (2.5); m₀, v and c in Einstein's equation; a continuity or a discontinuity, which is a property, not a value.

## Rules the sections keep

- A curve wears `function`; the level y = L it approaches is a dashed `limit` line, and the hole on the curve is drawn hollow in `function` with its height marked in `limit`.
- A one-sided limit is a `limit` with its side marked (an arrowhead from that side), never a second hue.
- In the squeeze, f and h are the bounds and g the function squeezed: three referents of one graph, each in its referent colour; the shared limit L wears `limit`.
- The secant wears `line` and the tangent `derivative`: the morph of one into the other changes hue as the secant arrives, which is the point.
- A function written only as a value ("f(2) = 0/0") is ink; the f in it wears `function`.
